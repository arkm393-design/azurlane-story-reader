    How this was made

    This site was built with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Claude wrote the reader, the design, and the pipeline that turns the game's files into scenes, including placing each character's expressions on their art. The site's maintainer directed the work, and compares each story with the game. Stories nobody has checked yet are listed separately, under Awaiting human review.

    Scenes are assembled automatically, so a few may differ from the game: a character slightly out of place, or an effect that isn't shown.

    Credits

    Azur Lane's story text, art and music belong to Manjuu, Yongshi and Yostar. This is a non-commercial fan project and isn't affiliated with them. The game data comes from two community projects, AzurLaneData and AzurAssets.
